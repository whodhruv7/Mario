T1Why Study Early Humans + Writing se Pehle vs Baad 🕰️☐ revised
📖 Definition (Hinglish)
Early human history — writing ke invent hone se pehle ka lamba period (insaani itihaas ka 99% se zyada hissa!), jise mainly archaeological evidence (tools, fossils, cave art) se padha jaata hai.
💡 Story Time
Socho ek aisi diary jo 3 million saal purani ho — but likhi nahi gayi! 😲 Toh historians ko pata kaise chala? Tools, bones, fossils — ye sab jaise time-capsule me band mile. Isi liye archaeology is period ki chaabi hai. Aur haan — likhai aayi toh sirf 5000 saal pehle, matlab humari poori kahani ka 99% hissa bina likhe guzra hai!
✍️ Points — yehi likhna hai
  • Early human history ka study batata hai ki biological evolution (australopithecines → Homo sapiens — physical changes) aur cultural evolution (Quaternary Period — last 26 lakh years — me tools/technology banakar environment ke saath adapt hona) sath-sath chale.
  • Life ka safar: hunting-gathering → agriculture → surplus food — aur surplus ne hi civilisation ki neev rakhi.
  • Likhai ke sources: Harappan script (pictographic — undeciphered, Sindhu lipi ke naam se bhi jaani jaati hai); cuneiform (Sumer) aur hieroglyphic (Egypt) — dono decipher ho chuki hain, inhi se ~5000 saal pehle historical period shuru maana jaata hai.
  • Brahmi script ~400 BCE se — southern India + Ganga valley; Aśhoka (3rd century BCE) ne ise formalise kiya.

T2Human Ancestors — Africa se Duniya Tak 🌍☐ revised
📖 Definitions (Hinglish)
Hominin — modern humans + human-like ancestors ka group (tool makers).  |  Fossil — purane plants, animals ya humans ke preserved remains/impressions, jo hazaaron-lakh saalon me dharti ke neeche dabe rehnaake patthar ban gaye.  |  Old World — Africa, Asia aur Europe (sabse purane settlements ka ilaka).
💡 Story Time
Poori insaani family ka ghar Africa tha! 🏠 Pehle Homo erectus nikla (~2 million yrs ago) apne handaxes aur cleavers lekar — Asia-Europe tak phail gaya. Phir ~125,000 yrs ago dusri wave nikli — hum khud, Homo sapiens (Africa me ~300,000 yrs ago evolve hue)! Aur tools? Unhe extra-corporal limbs kehte hain — matlab body ke "extra haath" jaise kaam karte the. 😄
✍️ Points
  • Sabse pehle stone tools ~3.3 million years ago bane — isi se 'human behaviour' shuru maana gaya (animals ye cognitive ability nahi rakhte).
  • Homo erectus = Africa se bahar nikla pehla hominin (handaxes + cleavers — tools Asia-Europe me mile).
  • Dusri badi exit ~125,000 yrs ago — early Homo sapiens; wo 50,000–12,000 yrs ago ke beech Australia aur Americas tak pahunche.
  • Archaeologists sites me se tools, bones dhoondhte hain aur khud similar tools banakar experiment karte hain — samajhne ke liye ki log unhe kaise use karte the.

T3Early History ke 6 Ages — Ek Oversh 👀☐ revised
💡 Story Time
Historians ne early history ko technology ke progress ke hisaab se 6 ages me baanta diya — kaunse tools, kaunsi lifestyle, kaisi settlements. Yaad rakhne ka trick: Pa-Me-Neo-Chal-Bronze-Iron — jaise phone ke updates, ek ke baad ek advanced version! 📱石器→🥉→⚙️
✍️ Points — 6 ages (tool + lifestyle basis)
  • Palaeolithic (Old Stone Age): hunting-gathering lifestyle; simple stone tools.
  • Mesolithic: microlithic (chhote patthar) tools; hunting-gathering se agriculture ke beech ka transitional phase.
  • Neolithic (New Stone Age): hunting-gathering se agriculture + settled life me shift; domestication of animals; polished stone tools.
  • Chalcolithic (Copper + Stone Age): stone ke saath copper ka use; early metallurgy (metal nikalna aur shape dena).
  • Bronze Age: bronze metallurgy (copper + tin milakar bronze); trade, towns aur early civilisations ka expansion.
  • Iron Age: iron metallurgy (smelting); stronger tools & weapons; zyada advanced societies.
🧠 Yaad rakhna: Ye division technological progress pe based hai — tools, agriculture ki shuruaat, lifestyle aur settlement patterns. Aur Neolithic Revolution = farming me shift (T6 me detail!).
T4Palaeolithic Life — Hunter-Gatherers 🪨☐ revised
📖 Definition (Hinglish)
Palaeolithic — palaeo = old + lithic = stone = Old Stone Age; hunter-gatherers ka daur, jab log shikaar aur khana khojkar jeete the.
💡 Story Time
Palaeolithic banda = survival ka full package khud! 😤 Handaxes se meat kaatna, scrapers se animal ki skin saaf karna, choppers se bones todo — andar se protein-rich marrow nikal khao! Phir aaye bow & arrow 🏹, cave walls pe paintings, body pe pigments, aur pehli beads (stone, bone, shell) — koi bhai/didi fashion se peeche nahi! 💅
✍️ Points
  • Indian subcontinent me sabse purani settlement ~2 million yrs ago; sites — Attirampakkam (Tamil Nadu, 1.5–1.7 mya) aur Isampur (Karnataka, 1.2 mya).
  • Tools: quartzite/limestone ke handaxes, cleavers, scrapers, choppers — meat kaatne, tubers khodne, skin scrape karne, bones se marrow nikaalne ke liye.
  • Middle Palaeolithic: chhote tools — scrapers, borers, points — projectiles (teer) banaake hunting efficient hui.
  • Later: bow & arrow; glassy rocks se blade & microblade tools (fresh banaye toh bahut sharp); small game ka shikaar.
  • Symbolic communication ka development, caves/rock shelters pe paintings, body decoration ke pigments, pehli beads, aur burin (engraver) — bones/shells (jaise ostrich eggs) pe design kaatne ka tool.
  • Ye developments Homo sapiens se judi hain — jo dheere-dheere poori duniya me phaile.
T5Mesolithic — Chhote Tools, Bade Changes 🎣☐ revised
💡 Story Time
~12,000 saal pehle Earth ne heater on kar diya! 🌡️ Climate warm hua — ice pighli, forests & grasslands phaile. Naye resources aaye: small game, fish, wild grains. Result? Itna khana ki insaani itihaas ki pehli population explosion hui! 🎉 Log machli pakadne lage (marine + freshwater dono) aur art bhi full on — Bhimbetka ki painted caves iska superstar hai.
✍️ Points
  • ~12,000 yrs ago climate warm hua — forests & grasslands pichle ice-covered ilaqon me expand hue.
  • Naye landscapes se mila: small game animals, fish, edible wild grains — waise hi pehli population explosion hui.
  • Microlithic (tiny stone) tools — aquatic food (marine + freshwater) jama karne me kaam aaye; fishing subsistence economy ki mainstay thi.
  • Art activity flourish hua; caves & rock shelters aksar occupy hue.
  • Bhimbetka (Madhya Pradesh) — World Heritage Site; hundreds of painted rock shelters — Mesolithic (aur usse bhi pehle) ke logon ke.
