🔑 Answer Key — Chapter 2
1 → b2 → b3 → c4 → b5 → a6 → c7 → c8 → d9 → b10 → c
1: Plate tectonics theory = W.J. Morgan (earth science).
2: Lithosphere = crust + upper mantle (≈100 km thick, broken into plates).
3: Core's heat → molten material rises, cooler sinks → convection currents push/pull plates.
4: Continental–continental convergence (convergent boundary) → fold mountains (Himalaya).
5: Ring of Fire = Pacific rim zone with most quakes + volcanoes (along plate boundaries).
6: Weathering = breakdown WITHOUT movement; erosion involves movement.
7: Plants/animals/micro-organisms = biological weathering.
8: Glaciers widen valleys into U-shapes; cirques, aretes, hanging valleys, fjords all glacial.
9: Waves erode weak rock → caves; caves on opposite sides meet → arch; arch collapses → stack.
10: GLOF = sudden release of water when the glacial lake's natural (ice/moraine) dam breaks.
